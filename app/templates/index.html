Garmin Account Connection

This platform uses taxuspt/garmin_mcp to securely access your activities, recovery metrics, and workout schedules without storing your Garmin password.

Step 1: One-time Terminal Authentication

Run this command in your Mac terminal (handles email, password, and MFA securely):

/Users/kachkarda/.local/bin/uvx --python 3.12 --from git+https://github.com/Taxuspt/garmin_mcp garmin-mcp-auth
Step 2: Token Storage

Tokens are stored in ~/.garminconnect. Once saved, both Antigravity MCP and this dashboard connect instantly.
